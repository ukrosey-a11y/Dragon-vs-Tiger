Frequently asked questions

Good to Know.

How do I order a service?

Select a service or pricing package, fill in the enquiry form and send your request through email or WhatsApp. We will confirm scope, availability and next steps.

Are the listed prices final?

No. Prices on this starter website are illustrative examples in USD, not a binding quote. The final price depends on scope, quantity, complexity, revisions and delivery time.

Can I request a custom package?

Yes. Describe the services and deliverables you need in the enquiry form. A custom scope can be discussed before the project starts.

Do you design websites and Shopify stores?

Yes, website design and Shopify storefront design are included in the service list. Platform access, subscriptions, paid themes, apps and development integrations may be separate costs.

How do revisions and delivery work?

Revision limits, delivery timelines and included file formats should be agreed in writing before work begins. They depend on the chosen project scope.

Can I pay directly on this website?

This version does not collect payments. Payment processing requires a configured payment provider and a secure backend or hosted checkout integration.
